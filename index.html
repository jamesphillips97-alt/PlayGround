✦FIELD NOTES

A little heat goes a long way.

Explore the ruins and collect all three ember seeds. Choose Pour lava, then tap nearby timber to set it alight. It takes a few moments to burn through.

Use Rebuild to place a plank wall, or Break to dismantle one and recover materials. Lava refills over time — keep an eye on the bucket.
